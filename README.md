#### Hello everyone!

Welcome to "Pawful-Situations", a lawless game with cats lives on the stake because YOU decided to play trivia tonight!
There's a twist! You are gambling your life away because these questions are NOT fair, and just like Squid Game, you truly did not know what you signed up for.

Prepare for wacky trivia questions that will have you scratching the door to be let out just like your annoying cat (or evil dog) who wants you to take them out for the 50th time today.

Here's who is on the game jam team: Jake (Producer & Systems + Backend Programming), Justin (Code), George (Code & Mechanics UI), Devon (Art),  Sam (Design)

## **WARNING** THIS GAME IS NOT, NOT NOT NOT NOT NOT FAMILY/WORK FRIENDLY. Trivia questions are not meant to be fair, and part of not being fair means no particpation trophies.
Adulthood is not fair and so we only recommend this game to people who life unfair lives, adults! I'm not joking.
====================

Let's talk how to play (given that it is a prototype):
1. Launch the game from the game scene. Currently, the bootstrapper -> main was meant to be for LAN networking (which works if you change the ip's and turn off your firewall!), but it is not included in the main game (yet).
2. Have everyone pick the decks they want to play with and then begin the game.
3. Answer the questions and hope that you get it right. Each player will go in ORDER, so designate each player to be P1-P4. If you get it right/wrong, hand over the computer to the NEXT person in order.
4. If you get it wrong, roll the die of DEATH (get it? die <-> death), and if you die, lose a cat life.
5. Lose all nine lives and you lose!

Note to all who made it this far: _have fun_
